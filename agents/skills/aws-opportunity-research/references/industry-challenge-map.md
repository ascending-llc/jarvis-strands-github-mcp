# Common Challenge Patterns by Industry

Use these as priors when classifying a target company's likely AWS-relevant challenges.
Confirm against evidence; do not assume.

- **Healthcare:** HIPAA compliance, data security, patient-data analytics, scalability
- **FinTech / Payments:** PCI-DSS compliance, real-time processing, fraud detection, security
- **Retail / E-commerce:** seasonal scalability, personalization, inventory, omnichannel
- **SaaS:** multi-tenancy, uptime/reliability, global distribution, cost optimization
- **Media / Gaming:** content delivery, real-time streaming, scalability, low latency
- **Manufacturing:** IoT data processing, predictive maintenance, supply-chain visibility

## Case-study relevance ranking

Rank candidate case studies before selecting:

- ★★★★★ same industry + similar challenge + similar size
- ★★★★ same industry + (similar challenge OR similar size)
- ★★★ adjacent industry + similar challenge, clearly applicable
- ★★ similar challenge + strong business outcomes
- ★ different industry but interesting approach

Select case studies of at least ★★★ relevance, preferring quantified business outcomes.
